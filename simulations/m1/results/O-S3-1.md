## Question

In Config S3-load, 11–28 % of the frames that a DRB's RLC TX entity accepted were never delivered by the peer RLC RX entity, and no statistic recorded them. Four candidate causes were tested: queueing, incomplete transmission at the end of the run, radio loss, and missing instrumentation.

## Answer

**Cause: a silent MAC discard.** When the MAC builds a transport block while all its HARQ TX processes are busy, it deletes the whole MAC PDU, together with the RLC PDUs it has already taken from the RLC. Simu5G 1.8.0 does this in `LteMacEnb::macPduMake` (gNB, DL) and `LteMacUe::macPduMake` (UE, UL):

```
if (txList.second.empty()) {
    EV << "macPduMake() : no available process for this MAC PDU in TxHarqBuffer" << endl;
    delete macPacket;
}
```

**Why the processes run out:**
- A TB's HARQ feedback arrives exactly 5 slots after its transmission (measured on all 4411 TBs of the trace), and both nodes have 5 HARQ processes (the NR default in Simu5G 1.8.0).
- Under saturation the UE is scheduled in every slot, so the scheduler keeps granting and the MAC keeps building PDUs while all 5 processes are waiting for feedback.
- The scheduler does not check for a free process before granting.

| Candidate cause | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete transmission at the end of the run | **Excluded** | Sources stop at 3 s and the run goes on to 5 s (Config O1-drain). The last frame of any flow is delivered at 3.146 s, yet 24 916 frames (seeds 0–4) are still missing 1.85 s later. |
| Queueing (overflow) | **Excluded** | No frame is lost before RLC (sent = RLC TX in, for every flow and seed). The MAC buffer-overflow statistic, which Simu5G emits on every MAC queue drop, never fired (0 of 20). The NR RLC UM TX buffer is unbounded. |
| Radio loss | **Excluded** | Every one of the 2426 DL and 1985 UL TBs was ACKed at its first transmission, with no NACK. HARQ error rate 0, 1 attempt per TB. |
| MAC discard when no HARQ process is free | **Confirmed, exactly** | Seed-0 trace. DL: 483 of 2909 MAC PDUs deleted, holding 2542 RLC PDUs. That equals the 15 529 RLC PDUs buffered at the gNB MAC minus the 12 987 extracted at the UE MAC, and equals the RLC PDU deficit in the recorded statistics. UL: 372 of 2357 deleted, holding 2438 = 15 261 − 12 823 = the statistics deficit. |
| Missing instrumentation | **Confirmed** | No recorded statistic shows the 4980 deleted RLC PDUs: MAC packet-loss, cell packet-loss and buffer-overflow are all zero or never emitted. The deletion appears only as a MAC debug-log line. |

**Counterfactual (Config O1-harq16).** With 16 HARQ processes at the gNB and the UE (NR allows up to 16; investigation only, not adopted):
- No MAC PDU is deleted, and every RLC PDU buffered at a TX MAC is extracted at the RX MAC.
- Missing frames drop from 12 222 UL / 12 694 DL to 0 UL / 5 DL (seeds 0–4).

**Per-flow pattern.** Loss per flow ranges from 0 % (DL PCP 0–4) to 27 %. Which frames fall into deleted PDUs depends on the phase of each 20 ms flow relative to the discard pattern. This was not analysed further.

## O-S3-2 (new, open): the last DL SDU of the run is never completed

With 16 processes, exactly one frame per seed is still missing: the last SDU to enter the gNB's DRB 1 (316 B, untagged, at 3.016006 s), in all five seeds.

**What the gNB RLC sent:**
- After that SDU arrived, the gNB RLC sent two PDUs, of 8 B and 314 B, and nothing more.
- A 314 B PDU cannot carry the whole 316 B SDU.

**What the trace shows** (seed 1):
- The UE's RLC completed the previous SDU (260 B) on the first of those PDUs.
- It buffered the second as an incomplete segment.
- No further PDU arrived.

**Interpretation (consistent with the data, not yet proven):** under load, each SDU's last few bytes travel in the next grant, together with the start of the next SDU. When traffic stops, the last tail is never requested.

**Why it matters:** this is about end-of-traffic behaviour, and possibly a one-grant delivery lag for segmented SDUs under load (relevant to M2 timing). It accounts for 1 frame per run here, not the 11–28 %.

## Consequences

- **S3 results stand.** The mapping checks never depended on delivery, and the light-load accounting (no loss) is unaffected. The delivery and delay figures of S3-load in `S3/experimental.txt` are shaped by this discard. They remain recorded without a claim.
- **For nascTime:**
  - In Simu5G 1.8.0, sustained load with TBs in every slot loses about 1 in 6 TBs per direction at the MAC, invisibly to the standard statistics.
  - Any M2/M3 reliability or latency result under load must either avoid this regime (for example by raising `harqProcesses`, a documented configuration choice) or count the discards from the trace.
  - Choosing a configuration is an M2 decision. Nothing is adopted here.
- **Upstream:** this is reportable to Simu5G as two items:
  - the scheduler grants without a free HARQ process, and the MAC then deletes data already taken from RLC;
  - the deletion is not recorded in any statistic.

  No upstream change was made.
