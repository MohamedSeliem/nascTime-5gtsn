### 2.1 Design choices

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| A new runner, not a mode of `m2s72s5.sh` | The plan's rule: new files, accepted runners byte-identical. The design §5.4 boundary is respected exactly. |
| The configuration set read from `tools/m2s72s5_gen.py matrix()` | The accepted, reviewed definition, not a copy. It includes `one0` N = 1 and Q-de (HARQ16), as generated for "every E1 combination". |
| The state machine is the accepted `m2s72_e1sel.Bracket`; the runner only replays `bracket.tsv` | One implementation of B-1 … B-4. A record that it refuses stops the batch. |
| `S5N_ADAPTER_VALIDATED` compared, never set; the decision record holds the value | Design §5.4 and T-no-d2-bypass. The executor exports it; the runner refuses on a mismatch. |
| Run-size and time limits are required inputs | No invented estimate. They are re-derived from the N = 2 smoke record (protocol §5.3). |
| Two segments (h16, h5), each one invocation into a fresh stage | The HARQ5 start (S5-2-D5) needs the **committed** HARQ16 record. Never overwriting means a stopped segment is restarted in a new root (L-4). |
| Expected-row completeness checked independently of the wrapper | A digest with a missing, extra or non-passing wrapper row, or a wrong `numUe`, is never a bracket result |

### 8.3 R3: authorization AG-1 (proposed; not approved)

**The mechanism (protocol §7.2)** is the smallest change to WP-A1 gate A:
- the decision record is signed by the maintainer: `ssh-keygen -Y sign -n nasctime-s5-e0vn-approval`;
- it is verified with `ssh-keygen -Y verify` for the principal `wpa1-maintainer`;
- the `allowed_signers` file holds exactly one key, obtained out of band; its fingerprint is recorded;
- the record, its signature and `allowed_signers` are copied into the stage's records.

The namespace differs from WP-A1's, so a gate-A statement cannot be replayed (tested).

**Refused (in the suite):**
- a missing signature or `allowed_signers`;
- a record altered after signing;
- a signature by another key;
- the WP-A1 namespace;
- another principal;
- two keys in `allowed_signers`.

**Approval status.** The mechanism is a proposal. `AUTH_GATE_APPROVAL = None`, so `gate production` refuses, and the runner refuses production `e0vn` and `select` even with a valid signature. Only a separately reviewed commit citing the maintainer's approval of AG-1 may set it. No tool or test sets it, and the suite asserts that it is `None`.

**Nothing is fabricated:**
- no approval exists;
- the development agent holds no maintainer key;
- no record is accepted for its form alone.
