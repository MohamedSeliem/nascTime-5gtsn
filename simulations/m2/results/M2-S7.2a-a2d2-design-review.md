## 1. Summary

**Why no run has ever NACKed.** The zero is structural, not luck.
- In the baseline channel, the UEs are at 50 m and 58 m. There, the DL SNR is about 64–66 dB.
- Simu5G's error model returns a block error rate of **exactly 0** above 35 dB (§2).
- No traffic level changes that: load is not an input of the decoding decision.

**The proposal.** Make one supported, upstream configuration change. Nothing in H-ARQ is touched.
- Run Simu5G's own `IdealChannelModel` at the gNB and both UEs. This is the channel Simu5G uses to exercise H-ARQ / RLC at a known loss rate (`simulations/nr/rlc/omnetpp.ini`, `[Config Base]`).
- Settings:
  - DL decoding-error probability **0.1 per attempt on ue[1] only**, with independent attempts;
  - ue[0] and all of the uplink loss-free.

**What stays the same.**
- N = 2 is kept, with its multi-UE attribution challenge.
- The baseline N = 2 smoke configuration is reused unchanged (`extends`).
- The effective configuration differs from it **only** by the 11 declared lines, plus the names (verified with `runconfig`, §5).

**The run.**
- One seed (0) and the baseline's 12 s.
- Wall-time limit 30 min (the N = 2 smoke took 195 s).
- Storage limit 2 × the N = 2 smoke's raw size.
- Distinct root: `$R/a2-nack-1`.

**Predicted outcome** (declared now):
- about **139 ± 24 real NACKs**, all on ue[1];
- about 125 retransmitted PDUs, about 12 with three or more transmissions;
- a real discard is unlikely (P ≈ 0.12). It is optional coverage, not a criterion (§6).

**Acceptance.**
- Required rows V-0…V-8 (§6.1), plus the frozen `a2-controls` and seven new real-evidence negative controls (§6.4).
- A **STOP** with no follow-up run if no NACK occurs (§7).

## 2. Source: how a DL decoding failure, its feedback and its retransmission arise (Simu5G 1.8.1, `7477d26`)

### 2.1 Decision chain (all line numbers in `src/simu5g/`)

1. **The UE PHY decides.**
   - `PhyUe::handleAirFrame` calls `channelModel->isReceptionSuccessful(frame, lteInfo)` (`stack/phy/PhyUe.cc:273`).
   - It attaches the boolean as `PhyReceptionInd` (`:293`).
2. **The UE H-ARQ RX stores it** (`LteHarqProcessRx.cc:60`). After `harqFbEvaluationTimer` slots (default 4; `LteMacBase.ned:38`), `LteHarqBufferRx::sendFeedback` sends ACK or NACK and logs `H-ARQ RX: feedback sent … result: NACK` (`LteHarqBufferRx.cc:77`).
3. **The gNB H-ARQ TX unit handles a NACK** (`LteHarqUnitTx::pduFeedback`).
   - Below `maxHarqRtx + 1` transmissions, the unit stays BUFFERED (`… PDU X set for RTX`, `:142`) and signals the MAC (`signalProcessForRtx`, `:146`).
   - At `maxHarqRtx + 1` it discards (`:124`). `maxHarqRtx` defaults to 3 (`LteMacBase.ned:37`); the project never sets it.
4. **Retransmission.**
   - The DL scheduler's `rtxschedule` runs only when a process awaits retransmission (`LteScheduler.cc:100`).
   - The same MAC PDU id is extracted again (`LteHarqBufferTx::sendSelectedDown`).
5. **Statistics.** Simu5G emits on the UE's NR MAC, per feedback (`LteHarqUnitTx.cc:165-195`):
   - `harqErrorRateDl` and `harqErrorRate_{1st..4th}_Dl`;
   - per ACK: `harqTxAttemptsDl`;
   - per ACK or discard: `macPacketLossDl`.

   Their `:mean` scalars are recorded.

**Nothing else produces a DL NACK.** The TX unit's only other NACK path, `selfNack()`, has **no caller** in 1.8.1 (check record §2). So every gNB NACK line is a PHY decoding failure, by construction. Queue overload, unused grants and RLC loss cannot create one.

### 2.2 Why the baseline cannot fail decoding

**The baseline channel.**
- The NR legs use `Tr38901ChannelModel` (`NrNicUe.ned:45`, `NrNicEnb.ned:39`).
- `fixedLos = dynamicLos = false` makes every link **NLOS** (`StochasticChannelModel.cc:1274`).
- Shadowing (σ 6 dB) and Jakes fading are on.
- `eNodeBTxPower` 40 dBm, `blerShift` 5.

**The error rule** (`StochasticChannelModel::isReceptionSuccessful`, `:1203-1206`):
- per allocated band, `snr > maxSnr()` gives `blockErrorRate = 0.0`;
- `maxSnr() = 40 − blerShift = 35 dB` (`PhyPisaData.h:46`);
- CQI feedback likewise saturates at 15 above 35 dB (`LteFeedbackComputationRealistic.cc:87`).

**Mean DL SNR** (TR 38.901 UMa NLOS, 2 GHz, before the σ 6 dB shadowing draw and fading):

| d2D | DL SNR | UL SNR |
|---|---|---|
| 50 m (ue[0]) | 65.8 dB | 53.8 dB |
| 58.3 m (ue[1]) | 63.7 dB | 51.7 dB |
| 340 m | 35.0 dB | 23.0 dB |
| 600 m | 25.4 dB | 13.4 dB |

**Consequences.**
- At the baseline positions, every band is about 30 dB above the table. Decoding cannot fail, at any load, which explains NACK 0 in every run so far.
- Load has no term in the decision. A larger transport block multiplies per-band success probabilities, `(1 − BLER)^RBs` (`:1215-1217`), and with BLER 0 that stays 1.
- What overload does produce is queueing, unused grants and RLC drops. None of them is a NACK.

### 2.3 The proposed mechanism: `IdealChannelModel` (upstream, unmodified)

- `isReceptionSuccessful` is `uniform(0,1) > per · harqReduction^(n−1)` for the n-th attempt (`IdealChannelModel.cc:51, 90`).
- `perDl` / `perUl` are per module and per direction (`IdealChannelModel.ned:56-57`).
- The reported SINR is pinned (`FAKE_SINR_DB`, `:24`), so CQI is 15 on every band. The baseline already sits at CQI 15 (§2.2).
- Upstream uses exactly this, with the same NrUe / gNodeB topology and the same coordinates, to drive H-ARQ and RLC at a known rate:
  - `harqReduction = 1`, `perUl = 0`;
  - the loss process on its own RNG (`simulations/nr/rlc/omnetpp.ini:81-96`).

## 3. Options considered

| Option | Supported | Controls NACK rate | Side effects | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. `IdealChannelModel`, `perDl` 0.1 on ue[1] only** | yes, upstream example | **exactly** (Bernoulli per attempt) | CQI pinned at 15 for both UEs (= baseline); UL and ue[0] loss-free; no geometry change | **recommended** |
| B. move ue[1] away (Tr38901) | yes | no: needs ≥ 340 m merely to reach the table, then depends on one shadowing draw, CQI / rounding mismatch and per-band fading; may give 0 NACKs (STOP) or near-total loss | **UL SNR falls with it** (13 dB at 600 m): UL H-ARQ NACKs and BSR / grant effects would be confounded; ue[1]'s CQI and TB sizes change | rejected |
| C. global `blerShift` / `targetBler` / `eNodeBTxPower` | yes | no | changes ue[0] too, plus every other link | rejected |
| D. more traffic | — | **no effect** on decoding (§2.2) | produces exactly the confounds to avoid | rejected |
| E. change H-ARQ logic, inject feedback | — | — | excluded by the task | excluded |

## 4. Why A avoids conflating decoding failures with other losses

- **NACK source.** By §2.1, every NACK is a PHY decoding failure. V-2 additionally requires that NACKs appear **only** where the design puts them:
  - ue[1] DL;
  - none on ue[0] (gNB and UE side);
  - none UL (gNB UL RX feedback and UE UL TX feedback; the latter read from the full MAC log).
- **No overload is added.**
  - ue[0]'s offered load and channel quality are unchanged: still CQI 15 and error-free.
  - ue[1] carries only its base flows (100 B / 20 ms + gPTP). That is about 1,250 TBs per run (§6.2), so 10 % retransmissions add about 0.1 TB/s.
  - With 16 processes, NACKed units cannot exhaust ue[1]'s H-ARQ space (expected peak 2–3 busy). The lifecycle classifier keeps the DL "no free H-ARQ process" text as an abort.
- **Discards and RLC.** A discard (expected 0.1 per run) loses its RLC PDUs. That is a consequence of H-ARQ, attributed by A-2 via the unit's discard line, never the reverse.
- **Unused grants.** The UE's "no free H-ARQ process for the grant" line concerns UL grants. With UL loss-free, it can only change through ordinary timing. It is reported, never read as a NACK.

**Effective configuration.** OMNeT++ 6.5.0 `runconfig` of the run against the baseline `M2S72-E0vN-q0-one0-n2-f1` (check record §5) shows:
- the 11 lines above (`num-rngs` 4 → 5);
- the configuration name in result paths;
- "changed lines other than these: (end)".

**Name.** The name keeps the E0v-N pattern only because the frozen A-2 CLI derives the process count from it (`parse_name`: HARQ16, nproc 16; check record). The `_A2V1` suffix and the separate root keep it out of every E0v-N ledger.

**What the extra RNG does.** It keeps the loss draws off RNG 0 (upstream practice). The run is **not** compared numerically with the baseline, beyond the reported ue[0] counts.

### 5.2 Choices fixed before the run

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `perDl` (ue[1]) | 0.1 | the conventional 10 % initial BLER operating point of link adaptation; about 139 NACKs expected, ≥ 9 sd above the floor of 30 |
| `harqReduction` (ue[1]) | 1 | independent attempts, so every count has a closed-form prediction (§6.2). Upstream's validation setting. With the default 0.3, three-transmission chains would be rare (about 4 per run) |
| `perDl` (ue[0]), `perUl` (all) | 0 | specificity (V-2) and no UL confound |
| seed, duration | 0, 12 s (baseline) | no change to the traffic or lifecycle the tools expect |
| discard | not targeted | `perDl` 0.3 would make discards likely (about 10), but at 30 % per-attempt loss. A discard is **optional coverage** (§6.3), and it is synthetically validated already. |

### 6.5 Uncertainties and declared risks

| Id | Risk | Effect | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R-1** | **A frozen-A-2 limitation found while designing this run**, and reproduced (T-v1-risk-R1): if the log ends between a NACK and its retransmission, A-2's replay correctly censors the unit ("awaiting retransmission"), but A2-0's precondition probe P0-3 reports "open at the end in state BUF", so A2-0 FAILs. **Likelihood:** OMNeT++ runs every event at t = 12 s before its end event (lowest priority, `csimulation.cc:85`). ## 7. Decision gates and STOP conditions

| Gate | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **G-0** (pre-run, static) | the preserved evidence hashes and the `a2-v1` / `a2-v3` / `a2-env-check` hash snapshot intact; branch / diff / frozen-A-2 identity; the `runconfig` diff = exactly the declared set; NC-V-baseline rc 4; free space ≥ 10 GB; `$R/a2-nack-1` absent | **any failure: no run** |

| **Science** | required rows and all controls | **ACCEPTED**: real NACK / retransmission chains validated, and the coverage class "real" is added for the NACK paths. Otherwise **NOT ACCEPTED**: a genuine finding, reported with the failing rows. |

**Lifecycle success ≠ scientific success.** L-1…L-4 only show that the run completed and its evidence is intact. The scientific verdict is the evaluator's, and it needs V-0…V-8 plus the controls.
