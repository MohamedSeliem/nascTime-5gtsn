## 8. Remaining scientific limitations

- **Simulation only:** OMNeT++ 6.4.0 / INET 4.7.0 / Simu5G 1.8.0, unmodified, with one topology (one gNB, one UPF, N ≤ 4 UEs, DL only, static roles). No physical-network or hardware evidence.
- **HARQ16 is an experimental control.** With Simu5G 1.8.0's default 5 HARQ processes, loaded UEs lose every sixth gPTP pair (P1). Block H correctness is shown only with HARQ16.
- **Narrow path heterogeneity.** It comes from one mechanism: S7.1 sustained load on one UE on the shared default DRB (Q-0). Q-d (UM) produces none. TT-clock heterogeneity (E-all) and path heterogeneity (H16, C-a only) are **not combined** in one configuration.
- **Different-Sync coverage is limited:** 0.75–6.5 % of samples per pair.
- **Seed variation is small.** Many outputs are deterministic across seeds (S7.1 seed-invariance finding). Five seeds are not five independent physical trials.
- **Hold neutrality is shown only for a single unlimited FIFO** without shaping or preemption (M2-S5 H5). The holds observed are about 10.864 µs serialization waits behind one 1,422 B frame. Other queueing disciplines are untested.
- **Weak GM identity:** the GM's sourcePortIdentity is all-zero, so association evidence rests on the ingress tag and syncFrameId.
- **MAC deletions are not recorded** in the record configurations. Their absence under HARQ16 is inferred from RLC PDU accounting; their presence with 5 HARQ processes was observed directly only in the P1 scratch twins.
- **No results for:** UL synchronization, RLC AM, N > 4, multiple gNBs, mobility, Stage 5 scaling or Stage 6 failure isolation.

**Stopped.** Stage 5 is not requested here; it awaits independent review of this record.
