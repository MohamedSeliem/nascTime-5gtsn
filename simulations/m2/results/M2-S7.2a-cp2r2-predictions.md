### 1.1 NC-cfout +1 ns: derivation from checker semantics

- **O5 reads the logged cfOut.** The path is `check_m2s5.cf_map` → `check_m2s6.Eval` :190–193.
- **Offset and step.** src = POT + cf + pd rises by 1000 ps, so diff = t2 − src falls by 1000 ps. The step servo sets new = now − diff (`slave_m2s6.py` :134–138), so the predicted slave clock after that update is +1000 ps.
- **M3 is predicted to fail.** The shift is exactly one 1 ns slave tick, so it survives quantization at the 1 ms samples. The rate change it induces is ≈ 8e-9, i.e. ≤ 1 ps over the interval. The measured clock is unchanged. M3 is therefore predicted to fail on the samples between that update and the next. This is derived, not assumed: the review asked for the M3 outcome to be confirmed from checker semantics.
- **M2 is predicted to fail.**
  - S row: gm and clock recordings ≠ O5.
  - E row C2: cf2 = cfOut(log) ≠ cf1 + add_res.
- **M3d is predicted to fail.** The per-slave identity holds, because terms and prediction move together by the representable 1000 ps. But (c) Σ path-specific = *measured* D_ij fails, because the prediction differs from the measurement.
- **I-3 is predicted to fail.** writeClock8(cfOut + 1000 ps) ≠ the wire value: whole ns are carried (`inet_model.inet_write_clock8`).
- **Predicted to pass:**
  - I-1 uses TSe and the Sync frames only.
  - F0 and I-2 do not read DS-TT 1's cfOut (`check_m2s72_f0.py` :146 reads the NW-TT cfOut).
  - The T rows do not read cfOut (`check_m2s6.t_rows`).
  - M4 uses Rr from Rm and r (`decomp_m2s6.py` :117–132), not cfOut.

### 1.2 NC-wirecf: derivation

- **I-3 is predicted to fail.** I-3 compares the slave's received cf_raw with writeClock8(logged cfOut).
- **Nothing else reads that field of a received Follow_Up.** O5 takes cf from the log (`cf_map`). The M2 R row reads csro of the received Follow_Ups, which is a different field. FCS validity is kept (`slave_m2s6.py` :41 requires it). F0 reads UPF PCAPs. The recorded slave clocks are simulation output and unchanged.

## 2. I-4 own stream: gate (decision 5)

**Row `I-4 stream`.** For opt ∈ {q0, qdu} and seed ∈ 0..4:
- DS-TT 1's TT-clock noise draws w (logged per stamp; test kinds 7–9 excluded, as they draw nothing) are compared in stamp order between `M2S72-B-<opt>-Eall-n2` and `-n4`.
- **PASS iff** the shorter sequence is a prefix of the longer, over at least 100 draws.

**Why DS-TT 1 only.** DS-TT 0 shares RNG 1 with the GM and the NW-TT (the M2-S6 mapping, kept for N = 1), so its draws can legitimately interleave differently with N.

**Historical evidence.** The Checkpoint 2 read-only diagnostic (`check/diag_m2s72_cp2.py`, `diag-cp2.txt`) is kept unchanged. Its per-update-interval comparison assumed one draw per update interval. In fact one draw is made per stamp (TtClock.cc:84). Its equal-at-common-instants result stands; its framing is superseded by this gate.

## 3. Block H: path heterogeneity (decision 6)

**Configs.** `M2S72-H-<q0|qdu>-Ca-n<2|4>`, seeds 0–4.
- **Source:** the S7.1 accuracy configs `M2S71-<opt>-Ca-sus`, i.e. C-a clocks (GM +100 ppm, slaves −100 ppm) and the S7.1 sustained load: PCP 0, 1400 B every 0.25 ms in each direction, on VID 42.
- **Load placement:** UE 1 only (asymmetric); UE 0 and UEs 2, 3 carry base flows only.
  - The listener side is the listener's last app, 3 + 2(N − 1), sending to talker[1].
  - The talker side is talker[1].app[3], sending to the listener.
- **DL load start:** D-4, 0.3 s + 500 µs. The UL load keeps the S7.1 offset, 0.1 s + 500 µs.
- **Inherited mixins:** M2S72-SL, the learning recorders and the eventlog.
- **No C++, no upstream change.** These are generated configs only.

**Rows, on every H run:**
- all the Block B rows: F0-1..8, I-1..I-4, M2, M3, M3d, M4;
- **I-5**, slave 0 vs `M2S72-B-<opt>-Ca-n1` with the same seed, rule AR.

**`H-cov` (gate, declared now).** Per H run and per pair (i, j) containing UE 1:
- (a) at least 10 % of the common Follow_Ups have Res_i ≠ Res_j;
- (b) at least one common Follow_Up has a non-zero pairwise GM-drift residence contribution (r − ρ_G)(Res_i − Res_j). Its exact reconstruction is required by the M4 GM-drift row of the same run.

**Reported, not gated:** s2 and s_F differences, and the case A / case B sample counts and proportions per pair. **If case B is absent or negligible, it is reported explicitly**, and a narrowly controlled timing perturbation is proposed for approval, not implemented.

**Trial runs.** Development trial runs of Block H (and of the controls) may follow this commit, to debug the generator and checker. They are disclosed in the record and kept. They do not change anything in this file.
