## 5. Scope: what is and is not established

**Established, on this one genuine run (one configuration, one seed, DL, two UEs, NACKs on ue[1] only):**
- A-2.1 attributes every gNB DL H-ARQ feedback line and accounts for every PDU lifecycle in the evaluation window.
- This holds both against the UE-side reference (XA) and against Simu5G's own per-UE statistics (A2-8).

**Two kinds of retransmission evidence, and what is missing:**
- *MAC selection evidence* (A2-4a): the MAC's "selected for retransmission" line for the process, in the same TTI. This line is logged under the MAC module.
- *UE-side reception evidence* (A2-4b): the retransmitted MAC PDU is received by the same node's UE.
- *Node-identified scheduler evidence* (A2-4c): **NOT EVALUATED**. Scheduler logging was disabled in the original configuration, so the sealed log has no `rtxAcid` line. A2-4a and A2-4b do not stand in for it, and its absence is never read as PASS. This is why the `full` contract is INCOMPLETE.

**Exercised in synthetic regression tests (corrected generator `m2s72_harqfix21.py`) but not evidenced in this genuine run.** Each item follows from the reported counts (no discards, NACKs on ue[1] only, end states only "awaiting feedback"):
- discards at maxHarqRtx (the genuine run has 0 discards);
- multi-NACK groups, ambiguous handles and narrowing by elimination (ue[0] never NACKs, so no group holds two NACKs);
- the UNRESOLVED discard failure;
- groups closed by counts with only RTX lines;
- structural censoring of a NACK or of a pending group at the end of the log (the genuine end states are only "awaiting feedback");
- A2-4c PASS and FAIL with scheduler logging on.

**Not covered at all:**
- UL H-ARQ;
- more than two UEs in a genuine run;
- other seeds, channel settings or schedulers;
- codeword 1;
- any claim about H-ARQ behaviour in general.

**Integrity provenance:** the seal hash `3d00ef8e…` was recorded at F3, not at the original sealing (D-F3-1).
