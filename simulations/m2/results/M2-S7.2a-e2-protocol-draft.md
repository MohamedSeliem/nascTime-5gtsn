# M2-S7.2a E2 load-sweep protocol, revision 1 (design only; NOT authorised to run)

Status: decision 2026-11-03 14:53, item 3 (option C.1). E2, E1, E3 and E4 are not authorised. This draft is for review; a run requires a separate authorisation and a predictions commit.

## 1. Purpose and limits

**Purpose.** Locate and characterise the DL saturation transition per N, placement and option, and the gPTP timing / reliability degradation around it. The comparison is matched and DL-only: Q-0 vs Q-d with **UL load disabled in both**.

**Why DL-only.** On Simu5G 1.8.0 the QoS-aware UL scheduler starves a backlogged UE. This is `LteSchedulerEnb.cc:403`, counterfactual `results/M2-S7.2a-cfqd-r2/`. UL-loaded Q-d runs would therefore compare a starved UL with a served one. Disabling UL load in both options removes that confound by design.

**This matrix cannot establish:**
- UL interference (UL load on shared N3/N6, NW-TT and listener links, or Pdelay on links shared with UL data);
- UL-direction timing;
- bidirectional dependability;
- any statement about the UL-loaded regime.

The E0 UL-loaded results (`results/M2-S7.2a-e0/`, `-e0r2/`) are unchanged, and stay the only UL-loaded evidence. DL-only results are not compared numerically with them, except for the explicit DL-only re-baseline at N = 1 (§3, step 0).

## 3. Adaptive matrix

**Step 0 — DL-only re-baseline at N = 1, one0, both options, F = 1 and 2.** Four runs. They measure what removing UL load changes against E0. The prediction is DL capacity and gPTP equal to E0 within stated tolerances, since DL and UL resources are separate at N = 1. Any difference is reported, not assumed away.

**Step 1 — coarse bracket**, per (N, placement, option):
- Factors in ascending order: **0.8, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 2.0 × L_tot**.
- Stop after the first factor that is overloaded by evidence (sustained growth on the load DRB **and** achieved DL < offered DL, E0 protocol §1), plus one factor above it.
- The N = 1 E0 capacity (≈ 1.21 × L_tot DL) is used only as the N = 1 expectation. It is never a threshold for N > 1, and every N is bracketed from 0.8.

**Step 2 — refine**, per (N, placement, option):
- Bisect [last non-overloaded, first overloaded] at most twice.
- Factors must give exact-ns intervals; the generator refuses others.
- Replicate the two factors around the transition at seeds 0–2.

**Step 3 — HARQ5 pairing:** one HARQ5 run per (N, option, placement) at the last non-overloaded factor, seed 0 (Simu5G-specific deletion behaviour; experimental). Q8 with the strict rule. An unattributable deletion is a stop condition, not a reclassification.

**Size:** step 0 = 4 runs; step 1 ≤ 4 N × 2 placements × 2 options × 7, typically about 5 each, so ≈ 80; step 2 ≤ 16 × (2 + 4); step 3 = 16. Upper bound ≈ 210 runs, typically about 150.

**Placement note.** sym at N = 1 does not exist (it is one0). The N = 1 bracket is shared by both placements.

## 4. Thresholds, onset and per-N reporting

**Thresholds and onset:**
- Exploratory onset thresholds and sensitivity as in the E0 protocol §5, unchanged.
- Onset order per (N, placement, option) is a falsifiable hypothesis, declared before step 1 of each N.

**Reported per N:**
- the measured transition factor;
- achieved-capacity plateau;
- HARQ busy-process maxima;
- censoring rate at the 12 s horizon (rates, never loss);
- timing degradation.

**Not reported:** numerical cross-N claims are made only from measured brackets, never extrapolated.

## 5. Provisional items carried

- **Q-d DL at N > 1 is provisional.** The same `:403` deactivation could affect a backlogged DL connection that receives no new SDU in a TTI. It is not observed at N = 1, where arrivals occur every TTI. The MAC-log active-set evidence will be reported per run so that such cases can be identified.
- **Stays out of scope until a separately authorised UL design:**
  - UL-loaded Q-d;
  - UL interference;
  - any bidirectional dependability claim.
- **The Simu5G fix** would enter only through an official release, adopted as a new baseline (contribution-plan rule). Candidate-build results are counterfactual evidence only.
