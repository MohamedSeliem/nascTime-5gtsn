## 2. What changed (`bcfbff5`; `git diff --stat 358654a bcfbff5`: 5 files)

**`tools/m2s72_gen.py` → `m2s72.ini`** (sha256 `0c684139…`):
- **Mixin `M2S72-SL`.** It contains exactly two lines:
  - `*.listener.eth[0].address = "0A-AB-00-00-00-01"`;
  - `*.upf.ethBridge.macTable.forwardingTable = [{address: "0A-AB-00-00-00-01", vlan: 42, interface: "dnEth"}]`.
- **Where it applies.** Every non-T0 config inherits it: the F0 census at N = 1, 2, 4, 8, with its `-ev` and `-norec` twins, and the controls.
- **Inheritance assertion** (`check_inheritance`). No `M2S72-T0-*` config may inherit `M2S72-SL`, an SL control mixin, `M2S72-L`, `M2S72-ev` or a D-4 mixin. Every non-T0 config must inherit `M2S72-SL`.

- **VLAN-42 assumption.** An F0 source must have `**.app[0..1].tagger.vlanId = 42` and no flow on another VLAN.
- **Ownership.** A source may not set the listener MAC or any UPF MAC-table parameter, since `M2S72-SL` owns them.
- **`M2S72-ev`** (eventlog twins). It now also records the INET log lines of `upf.ethBridge.macTable` only, in non-express mode. This is needed for S2.
- **Control mixins** `M2S72-SLN-{missing,vlan,age,port}`, and the configs `M2S72-F0N-*` with their `-ev` twins.
- **Unchanged:** T0 configs, D-4 (0.3 s), `omnetpp.ini`, M1Bridge and every earlier oracle.

**A disclosed development iteration of the port control.** A first, uncommitted version only gave `talker[1]` the listener's MAC.
- Its uplink flows were then addressed to its own MAC and never left the host, so no listener-MAC frame entered a session.
- It failed F0-4, F0-5 and F0-6 instead of the predicted F0-4, F0-6 and F0-8. It did not exercise the case.
- It was redesigned before the record so that `talker[1]` sends its uplink flows to `talker[0]`. This makes listener-MAC VID-42 frames enter on session 1.
- The census gates were unaffected and nothing was reclassified. The trial output is kept outside Git.

## 6. Caveats

1. **The configuration-initialised entry is not protected in INET 4.7.0.** It is inserted at −1 s, can be moved by learning, and ages at agingTime − 1 s. F0-8 and S3/S5 gate exactly these properties for every run. The `age` and `port` controls show they are detected.
2. **S2 relies on INET's own `MacForwardingTable` log text**, read from the eventlog twin in [0, 0.5 s]. The twin is proven identical to the census run in every output and fingerprint (F0-7).
3. **N = 1 carries the static configuration too** (approved). There, flooding has nowhere to go, and the fingerprints are unchanged from Checkpoint 1.
4. **The effect of the Checkpoint 1 floods on gPTP timing** was shown only in the scratch probe (Q-0: 7/46 residences at N = 2, 43/184 at N = 8). It is not part of this record, since there is no flooding to attribute.
