## 1. Labels and verdicts (fixed in advance)

- **Original verdict: A2-V1 NOT ACCEPTED. It stands permanently.** S1 never changes, relabels or reclassifies it, or any record under `/mnt/nasctime-results`.
- **The S1 verdict is separate.** It is labelled **"A2-V1-S1 (supplementary; A-2.1, contract F1-d(i))"** and takes one of four values: ACCEPTED, NOT ACCEPTED, INCOMPLETE or STOP (integrity).
- **The full contract is reported but is never an acceptance basis here.** A2-4c needs scheduler logging, which the sealed run does not have, so it is **NOT EVALUATED** and the `full` contract gives **INCOMPLETE**. S1 ACCEPTED means accepted under F1-d(i) only, and must always be stated that way.

## 2. Inputs, read-only

The inputs are those `check_m2s72_a2.inputs` uses, the same function the original A2-V1 evaluation called:
- the log `runs/run-<cfg>.log.gz`;
- the grants (parsed by `m2s72_maclog`);

The window is the tools' default, t0 = 2 s, the same as in the original A2-V1 evaluation. All outputs go to a fresh directory `$S1` outside `/mnt/nasctime-results`, and `TMPDIR` points inside `$S1`. The in-memory scalar control writes its temporary `.sca` copies there.

## 5. Stop conditions

- **G-0 fails** (hash, code, pin or suite): stop before I-1. No evaluation.
- **I-1 fails:** stop. No evaluation. Report.
- **E-1 or E-2 gives rc 2** (evaluation error), or the VM kills a step: stop. No second attempt, no change of inputs, no partial verdict.
- **I-2 fails:** the S1 verdict is **STOP (integrity)**, whatever E-1…E-3 showed. Report it as an integrity event on the sealed run.
- In every case: keep `$S1` as it is, never re-run into it, and never edit a record. Any new attempt needs a new decision and a new `$S1`. A deviation from §4 is reported as found. No expectation, row or record is adjusted after seeing results.
