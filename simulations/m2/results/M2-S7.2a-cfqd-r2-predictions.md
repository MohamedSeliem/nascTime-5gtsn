## Part B — strict E1–E5 exclusion rule adopted (opt-in `Q8(attr="exclusion")`, multi-UE runs)

**Adoption change (fail-closed).** A candidate UE whose RLC RX vector is **absent** is no longer treated as "all missing". The rule now refuses it as **missing reception evidence (E3)**. Before, an absent vector would have looked like an all-missing UE.

**Unchanged:**
- E1, E2, E4, E5;
- the open-window part of E3;
- no use of logging order;
- ambiguous, partly received or under-evidenced TTIs are UNEXPLAINED.
