## 7. Batch rules

1. **Order and escalation (E0 [A]):** factors 1, 2, 4, 8 ascending; Q-0 then Q-d at each factor. Stop after a factor at which the cohort Syncs not received by T_end exceed 50 % in Q-0 **and** Q-d (E0's `quick` proxy); then run the twin of the highest Q-0 factor run.
2. **One simulation at a time.** Per-run free-space rule (plan rev. 1 §4.1): free ≥ 1.5 × the run estimate + the twin estimate + 1 GB checker + 2 GB margin. Run estimates (E0 sizes × 1.2, including logs): 0.9 / 1.2 / 1.7 / 2.7 GB for factors 1 / 2 / 4 / 8.
3. **Evidence lifecycle** (RD-5): per run, in this order:
   1. outcome;
   2. per-run check;
   3. manifest (sha256 of every raw file, fsync'd);
   4. ledger;
   5. tier action:
      - **tier B** (kept, `gzip -n` in place, verified against the manifest) for a non-OK outcome, a failed check or a failed non-perturbation;
      - **held** while a later step still needs the run: controls, the twin;
      - **tier C** otherwise.

   Tier-C raw outputs are deleted **only after** the run's records are committed to the records repository (`S5_RECORDS_REPO`) and verified in its HEAD. Without a records repository they stay, pending `release`.
4. **Records committed per batch:**
   - `env-<mode>.txt` and `stack.txt`;
   - `runs.tsv` (config, seed, outcome, rc, wall time, raw bytes, check, manifest sha256, tier);
   - per run: `.check.txt`, `.digest.json`, `.manifest.sha256`, console;
   - `controls-*.txt`, `nonpert-*.txt`;
   - `ledger.tsv`, `steps.txt`.
5. **Recovery of a deleted run:** deterministic re-run from the recorded commit, configuration and seed, in the **same environment**, verified against the committed manifest's PCAP and per-frame log hashes. `.sca` / `.vec` embed run metadata and are not byte-reproducible.

## 8. Environment

The E0v records are bound to the environment named by `S5_ENV_DECISION` and recorded in `env-*.txt` and `stack.txt`. Comparisons with the 1.8.0 E0 record are cross-version and expected to differ (F1–F5 of the plan); they are reported as differences, never as failures.

**D-2.** The reference environment is OMNeT++ 6.5.0 / INET 4.7.0 / Simu5G 1.8.1 (unmodified), conditional on:
1. the G0 environment-equivalence record;
2. S7.2a CP1 and CP2 tested independently on that environment.

When both conditions are met and reviewed, the value to set is:

`S5_ENV_DECISION="D-2 2026-11-15 12:58: OMNeT++ 6.5.0 / INET 4.7.0 / Simu5G 1.8.1; G0 record <commit>; CP1/CP2 <record commit>"`

with the commits filled in. A run with any other stack is not a D-2 result.

**G0-R1 (found 2026-11-15, before any Stage 5 run).**
- OMNeT++ 6.5.0 `opp_run -q runconfig` prints only the first, effective entry per key. 6.4.0 printed the whole inheritance chain, including shadowed entries.
- Consequence for the accepted `m2s72_gen.py verify` (flattening identity): it reports DIFFERENT for all nine source configurations on 6.5.0, although every effective entry is equal. That verdict is kept as it is.
- The version-independent companion `tools/m2s72_verify_eff.py`:
  - compares the effective entries, keys, values and order;
  - reports IDENTICAL for all nine;
  - its controls detect one changed value, one dropped key and one swapped pair, and ignore a changed shadowed entry.
- No checker parses runconfig.
- The Stage 5 `verify` / `diff` compare effective configurations on either output form. They passed on 6.5.0.

## 9. Smoke run (prepared; not authorised)

**Purpose:**
- the first reviewed 1.8.1 MAC-log grammar census (MG-1, "frozen from the first reviewed run", §4);
- the run's raw size and wall time in the D-2 environment, from which the §7 estimates and limits are re-derived before E0v;
- one pass of the lifecycle steps on real output.

It is **not an E0v result**:
- `e0v` reruns `M2S72-E0v-q0-f1` in its own stage;
- no prediction of §3 is evaluated on it;
- its per-run check verdicts are reported for review only.

**Runner mode `smoke`.** Order of steps:
1. free-space rule (run estimate 0.9 GB, no twin);
2. the one simulation, with a fixed 60 min limit;
3. outcome classification;
4. per-run check;
5. manifest (sha256 of every raw file, fsync'd);
6. the E0 `quick` proxy;
7. `controls-q8` (in memory; the raw files are only read);
8. `chmod -R a-w` on the run's raw outputs;
9. manifest re-verification;
10. ledger `held (smoke review …)`.

**Retention (never deleted):**
- The raw outputs stay **uncompressed and read-only**.
- No `pending-release.txt` is written. `release` refuses the smoke stage.
- `S5_RECORDS_REPO` is ignored.
- The stage directory can never be reused (`nt_fresh_out`).
- Records go to `<root>/m2/M2-S7.2a-s5smoke/records/`.

**Exit codes:**
- 0: clean;
- 3: free-space stop before the run;
- 5: non-OK outcome; the raw outputs are kept as tier B by the common path;
- 6: any of check / quick / controls / manifest not clean; every later step is still done and recorded.

**After review:** the grammar census is frozen into MG-1 by a separate, reviewed commit before E0v. The smoke raw outputs stay until you decide; the options are tier B (`m2s72s5_lib.py retain <manifest>`) or keeping them as they are.

The exact commands are in `M2-S7.2a-s5-vm-procedure.md` §5.
