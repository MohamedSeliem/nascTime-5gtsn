## 7. Batch rules

1. **Order and escalation (E0 [A]):** factors 1, 2, 4, 8 ascending; Q-0 then Q-d at each factor. Stop after a factor at which the cohort Syncs not received by T_end exceed 50 % in Q-0 **and** Q-d (E0's `quick` proxy); then run the twin of the highest Q-0 factor run.
2. **One simulation at a time.** Per-run free-space rule (plan rev. 1 §4.1): free ≥ 1.5 × the run estimate + the twin estimate + 1 GB checker + 2 GB margin. Run estimates (E0 sizes × 1.2, including logs): 0.9 / 1.2 / 1.7 / 2.7 GB for factors 1 / 2 / 4 / 8.
3. **Evidence lifecycle** (RD-5): per run, in this order:
   1. outcome;
   2. per-run check;
   3. manifest (sha256 of every raw file, fsync'd);
   4. ledger;
   5. tier action:
      - **tier B** (kept, `gzip -n` in place, verified against the manifest) for a non-OK outcome, a failed check or a failed non-perturbation;
      - **held** while a later step still needs the run: controls, the twin;
      - **tier C** otherwise.

   Tier-C raw outputs are deleted **only after** the run's records are committed to the records repository (`S5_RECORDS_REPO`) and verified in its HEAD. Without a records repository they stay, pending `release`.
4. **Records committed per batch:**
   - `env-<mode>.txt` and `stack.txt`;
   - `runs.tsv` (config, seed, outcome, rc, wall time, raw bytes, check, manifest sha256, tier);
   - per run: `.check.txt`, `.digest.json`, `.manifest.sha256`, console;
   - `controls-*.txt`, `nonpert-*.txt`;
   - `ledger.tsv`, `steps.txt`.
5. **Recovery of a deleted run:** deterministic re-run from the recorded commit, configuration and seed, in the **same environment**, verified against the committed manifest's PCAP and per-frame log hashes. `.sca` / `.vec` embed run metadata and are not byte-reproducible.

## 8. Environment

The E0v records are bound to the environment named by `S5_ENV_DECISION` and recorded in `env-*.txt` and `stack.txt`. Comparisons with the 1.8.0 E0 record are cross-version and expected to differ (F1–F5 of the plan); they are reported as differences, never as failures.
