# M2-S3: offline gPTP correction oracle (results)

**Verdict: PASS.** All 24 rows of `M2-S3/check.txt` pass. Regressions (M2-S1, M2-S0, M1) are unchanged (Regressions section).

**What this stage is.** An offline oracle over the recorded M2-S1 runs, implementing `migration/m2-s2-design.md` section 6 (revision 2, with the revision 2.1 errata that this stage found). It runs no simulation, modifies no packet, implements no translator Pdelay and no online correction, and changes no simulation code, ini or upstream source.

**Main result (ideal-clock baseline only).**
- An independent re-implementation of the INET 4.7.0 gPTP slave reproduces the recorded slave **exactly**, in 15 runs. This covers every pdelay, neighborRateRatio, gmRateRatio and compensation value, and all 76 189 clock samples (C1). It is shown to be sensitive to each modelled rule (C3).
- Fed the recorded traffic with `cf' = Lup_ref + Res` and `pdelay = Ldn_ref`, that model predicts a slave clock error of **0 ps exactly** after every one of 500 steps, at 487 Sync arrivals and at 63 577 periodic samples (D2). The same holds from an injected initial offset (D2b).
- Each of seven listed mistakes produces its predicted error exactly (NC-a to NC-f). One predicted signature in the design had the wrong sign and is corrected (NC-b, below).

**Scope of the claim.**
- The recorded Sync/Follow_Up/Pdelay traffic, processed by a model shown to reproduce INET's slave exactly, would be synchronized with zero error by the section-3 composition. This holds for ideal clocks, reference link delays and rr = 1.
- **No claim** about a Pdelay implementation (the 50 ns are reference values), non-unit rate ratios (rr = 1 is the baseline only), wire fidelity, synchronization accuracy, transparent-clock behaviour or IEEE 802.1AS compliance. All 802.1AS/1588 semantics remain unverified (design section 0.1).

## Inputs

- **Runs** (recorded M2-S1 runs, `results/M2-S1/runs`):
  - **Primary:** M2S1 seeds 0–9, M2S1-frag, M2S1-load and M2S1-load-harq16.
  - **Controls (rows A and C only; no translator logs):** M2S1-off and M2S1-load-off.
- **Observation points:** PCAP (pcapng, 1 ps) at the GM (`listener` eth[0]), NW-TT eth[1], DS-TT eth[1] and the slave (`talker` eth[0]).
- **M2-S1 port logs:**
  - t1: NW-TT eth[1] ingress;
  - t9 and t12: DS-TT eth[1] egress;
  - t5 and t8: the 5G-side ports, for B.
- **Slave's recorded vectors:** `talker.gptp` pdelay/neighborRateRatio/gmRateRatio; `talker.clock` timeChanged/oscillatorCompensationChanged.
- **Provenance (A1):** all 112 PCAP and log files read are hash-identical to `M2-S1/raw-outputs.sha256` at `56c8fb3`. The `.vec` files are hashed in `M2-S3/vec.sha256`; they are not byte-reproducible because of run attributes such as date and process id.

## Results (`M2-S3/check.txt`)

### A. Inputs, decoding, serialization

| Row | Result |
|---|---|
| A1 | 112/112 files match |
| A2 | Every gPTP frame at the four points decodes with the INET serializer layout: versionPTP 2, domain 0, FCS valid. messageLength: Sync 44, Follow_Up 76, Pdelay messages 54. Follow_Up TLV: type 3, length 28, organizationId 0x0080C2, subtype 1, csro 0. Census in `census.txt`. **Finding:** INET writes Sync flags `0x0002`, i.e. bit 1 of the *second* flag octet. Whether this is the 1588/802.1AS twoStepFlag position is unverified. |
| A3 | Payload bytes are identical on every cable. Across the 5GS, the delivered messages are an in-order, byte-identical subsequence: no element on the path is time-aware. **Lost or in flight at the end of the run:** 25 DL messages (load runs: Sync/Follow_Up; load-harq16: one Pdelay_Resp + Resp_Follow_Up) and 14 UL. |
| A4 | Wire POT = floor_ns(GM Sync `transmissionStarted`); wire t2 = floor_ns(GM Pdelay_Req `receptionStarted`); wire t3 = floor_ns(GM Pdelay_Resp `transmissionStarted`), on every message. **Sub-ns residues lost on the wire:** POT 0/585, t2 76/76, t3 72/76. correctionField is 0 on all 5 570 frames. |
| A5 | The oracle's encoders/decoders equal INET's **real** `GptpPacketSerializer` on 41 synthetic vectors (`serializer-probe.txt`; `check/inet_probe` links the unmodified libINET). |

**INET 4.7.0 serialization findings (executed, not just read):**

| Field | INET behaviour |
|---|---|
| correctionField write (`writeClock8`) | Whole ns << 16. **The sub-ns part is always dropped.** Example: 4 999 114 800 ps is written as 4 999 114 ns; −1 500 ps as −1 ns (truncation toward 0). |
| correctionField read (`readClock8`) | Decodes the 2⁻¹⁶ ns fraction, floored to ps. Examples: 0x8000 → 500 ps; 0xCCCD → 800 ps; 66 → 1 ps; 65 → 0 ps. |
| Timestamps | 48-bit s + 32-bit ns, truncated. Example: 125 000 000 200 ps is written as 125 000 000 000 ps. |
| cumulativeScaledRateOffset | ceil((rr − 1)·2⁴¹). Examples: rr = 1 + 0.5·2⁻⁴¹ → 1; rr = 1 − 0.5·2⁻⁴¹ → 0. INET's comment says "truncated". The 802.1AS rule is unverified. |

### B. Timing identity (consistency only)

| Row | Result |
|---|---|

### C. Independent INET-slave model (`check/inet_model.py`)

| Row | Result |
|---|---|
| C1 (sim mode: wire fields + residue restored from the GM's own event times) | Exact on 15/15 runs: 75 pdelay, 60 nRR, 557 gmRateRatio and 572 compensation values; 572 steps; 76 189 clock samples, incl. the old value at every step. Values are compared as the double the vector recorder writes (`SimTime::dbl`); below 2⁵³ ps that is ps equality. |
| C2 (wire mode: decoded fields only) | Wire mode deviates from the recording **exactly** in the two runs where a residue does not cancel: M2S1-load and M2S1-load-off. In those runs, 4 pdelay samples differ by −18 to −8 ps and 27 steps by −28 to −7 ps. The deviation is predicted independently of the model, from the decoded residues (t3 − t2 residue ≠ 0 on 2 exchanges; t3 residue change on 3). In the other 13 runs, t2 and t3 carry the same residue, so t3 − t2 is exact and wire = sim. **Finding:** if INET serialized gPTP inside the simulation, the current slave's values would change by tens of ps under load. |
| C3 (mutation controls) | Each single-rule mutant fails C1 on 15/15 runs: smoothing rounding, compensation/origin order, nRR omitted, rate ratio via double quotient, Sync timestamp at last bit. The one mutant predicted in advance to be indistinguishable (Pdelay t1/t4 at last bit: Pdelay_Req and Pdelay_Resp have equal length) is indeed not detected (0/15). That rule is therefore **not validated** by these runs. |

## Deviations from the approved design (revision 2), recorded as design revision 2.1 errata (design section 6.1)

| Item | Approved | Implemented | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| NC-b signature | −(pdelay₁ − 50 ns) | **+(pdelay₁ − 50 ns)** | Sign error in the design. The slave sets its clock to POT + cf + pdelay at Sync reception, so a pdelay that is too large sets the clock ahead. The oracle's result follows that derivation. Section 5 point 3 is corrected the same way. |
| C2 condition | Deviation iff a sub-ns residue exists | Iff a residue does not cancel in t3 − t2, between t3 values, or in POT | Residues exist in every run (t2/t3), but in 13 runs they cancel. The refined condition is predicted from the decoded residues, not from the model. |
| A2 twoStep flag | Decoded | Recorded, not required | INET writes 0x0002. The flag position in 1588/802.1AS is unverified. |
| A3 transport | Identical GM → … → slave | Identical per cable; in-order subsequence across the 5GS | Messages are lost or in flight in the 5GS under load. |
| Additions | — | A5 (real-serializer probe), C3 (mutation controls), D2b (injected offset) | Stronger independent evidence; no change to any criterion. |
| D3 | Report loss; state "not exercised" if Res has no sub-ns part | As designed: **not exercised** | No sub-ns part in any Res or POT. |
| Probe program | — | `check/inet_probe` (C++, links unmodified libINET, no simulation) | Grounds A5 in INET's code execution. It is built outside `src/nasctime` and is not part of `libnasctime_m2`. |

## Limitations

- **Ideal clocks.** rr = nRR = 1. Rate-ratio handling is not validated; NC-e only shows that applying rr ≠ 1 with ideal clocks is detected.
- **Pdelay.** 50 ns are reference values. The rule "Pdelay t1/t4 at first bit" is not distinguishable on these runs (C3).
- **Encodings.** Encoding-loss behaviour is not exercised (D3).
- **Simulation-only carriage.** The prediction assumes the Follow_Up carries cf' as an unserialized INET chunk value (simulation-only, option B). Wire carriage and the Suffix TLV (option C) are not covered.
- **Standards.** Every 802.1AS/1588 statement remains unverified (design section 0.1). The 802.1AS edition decision is still open.

**Independent reproduction of the oracle.** The oracle was run on two independent generations of the M2-S1 runs:
- the original runs (`.vec` hashes equal to `M2-S1/raw-outputs.sha256`);
- the runs regenerated by the regression rerun (new `.vec` files; PCAP and logs hash-identical).

`check.txt`, `census.txt`, `syncs.csv`, `slave-model.txt` and `serializer-probe.txt` are byte-identical between the two. The committed `vec.sha256` belongs to the regenerated runs, so it reports them as differing from the committed M2-S1 hashes.

**Fresh-clone reproduction from the bundle.** Steps:
1. Created a clean repository containing only `56c8fb3` (none of the M2-S3 commits present).
2. Verified `nascTime-m2-s3.bundle` and imported it fast-forward (5 commits, up to `eb77354`).
3. Ran `m2s1.sh` in that clone: a third independent generation of the runs, PASS 28/28.
4. Restored the committed M2-S1 records and ran `m2s3.sh` (PASS 24/24).

`check.txt`, `census.txt`, `syncs.csv`, `slave-model.txt` and `serializer-probe.txt` were byte-identical to the committed records; only `env.txt` and `vec.sha256` differ, as expected.
